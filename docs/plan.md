# Khazuk!: the Dwarfs, and the player is the Ancestor Gods

The user, 2026-11-01: "i was thinking about dwarfs soon", "normal dwarfs not chaos", and to start them after the Waaagh!'s W5. This is
the third race on Settlement Core, and the second on Minecraft 26.1.2. Its id is `khazuk`, after the Dwarf war cry ("Khazukan
Kazakit-ha!"), as the Waaagh! is named after the Orks'. Everything below is my pick until the user changes it (section 12).

## 1. The premise

- **The player is the Ancestor Gods:** Grungni the miner and smith, Valaya the hearth-keeper, and Grimnir the warrior. The Dwarfs revere
  their ancestors, so the god they build for is you, as the Orks fight for Gork.
- **The hold, a karak, is dug into the ground,** round an **Ancestor Shrine**: the race's heart block, a statue of the three Ancestors in
  stone and gold. Set down, it is its placer's, and Dwarfs come up from the deeps to it.
- Dwarfs come up from the deeps as the Orks come up from the spores: out of a stair shaft by the Shrine, not out of the ground.

## 2. The hold, dug and raised

- **Dug:** the engine's dig side gets its first 26.1 race. Miners dig the hold's halls under the Shrine from the god's plans:
  - the Great Hall;
  - mines that follow ore;
  - the forge;
  - the brewery;
  - the barracks;
  - the treasury;
  - the deep roads between holds.
- **Raised:** a gatehouse and walls on the surface (the engine's surface builder and walls, as the Waaagh!'s palisade uses them),
  stone-built and square.

## 3. Gold, ale and runes

- **Gold** is the money: mined from gold ore (the engine's mined currency), paid for plans and units, and hoarded in the treasury.
- **Ale** is the morale. A brewery brews it from wheat; a hold with ale in its barrels works faster and fights harder. Buffs only:
  dry, a hold is just a hold.
- **Runes** are the buffs:
  - a Runesmith at the runic forge strikes runes into the hold's kit and onto its walls and gates;
  - a rune is permanent and stacks to three, as in the lore (runic armour, runic weapons, a rune of warding on a gate).

## 4. The Book of Grudges (Dammaz Kron)

The Dwarfs' signature. The book lies on the Shrine, and the hold writes in it:
- whatever kills a Dwarf;
- whatever breaks the hold;
- whatever steals its gold.

Each grudge names a foe: a kind of mob, a named one, or a player who wronged them. **Settling** one (krumping that foe, or that many of
its kind) is rewarded:
- gold, ale, a rune;
- the hold's Dwarfs hit that kind harder from then on ("grudgebearers").

An open grudge costs nothing (buffs, not nerfs). Old grudges still count ("the Book does not forget").

## 5. The Ancestor Gods' powers

The god's powers, paid from the hold's devotion (prayers at the Shrine, grown by settled grudges), stronger with a Runesmith near:
- **Grungni's Hammer:** a forge-hammer of light strikes where the god points (the Dwarf answer to the Foot of Gork).
- **Valaya's Ward:** a shield over the hold or a host; blows and fire turned aside for a while.
- **Grimnir's Fury:** the Slayers' rage for every Dwarf near, no fear and harder blows.

## 6. The units, in TWW1's order (the Dwarfs are a TWW1 race)

- **First:** Miners (the workers: dig, mine, haul, and fight with picks) and Dwarf Warriors (the soldiers: hand weapon and shield).
- **Then, in TWW1's roster order:**
  - Quarrellers and Thunderers;
  - Longbeards;
  - Ironbreakers and Hammerers;
  - Slayers (Troll Slayers, then Giant and Dragon Slayers);
  - Irondrakes;
  - the war machines: the Grudge Thrower, the Cannon, the Organ Gun, the Flame Cannon;
  - the Gyrocopter and Gyrobomber.
- **The first showpiece (the user's pick, 2026-11-01): Thorgrim's Throne of Power, as the player's own mount** ("his throne would be
  first, but as a rideable for me"):
  - the player sits where Thorgrim sits, four Throne Bearers carry it, and the Book of Grudges rides on it;
  - the detail session models it. The mod gives a rideable entity the player steers like a horse, at the seat point the model's
    contract names;
  - its clips are idle, walk and run (the bearers in step), kneel and rise (to mount and dismount), a bearer's kick or barge when the
    rider attacks, and the Book opening when a grudge is written or settled (D4);
  - its size is the user's to pick.
- **Heroes and lords:** the Thane, the Runesmith, the Master Engineer, the Dragon Slayer; the Dwarf Lord, the Runelord; a King for a hold
  that grows into a capital.
- **Strength:** per stat, the higher of the army book and TWW3's battle stats (the user's rule). A Dwarf is short, broad, tough and
  slow: high armour and health, steady, no running away. The floor for a Dwarf Warrior is set when D2 measures him; for a start, he beats
  three zombies, as an Ork Boy does.
- **Looks:** TWW3's models first, then GW's miniatures. Miners and Warriors are fast models; heroes, lords and war machines are
  showpieces for the detail session.

## 7. Engine needs
